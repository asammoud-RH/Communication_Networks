# Modulation Visualizer

Learn how analog and digital modulation differ, how ASK (Amplitude Shift Keying), FSK (Frequency Shift Keying), PSK (Phase Shift Keying), and QAM (Quadrature Amplitude Modulation) put bits on a carrier wave, why denser schemes need a cleaner signal, how Wi-Fi uses OFDM (Orthogonal Frequency-Division Multiplexing), and how spread spectrum lets GPS (Global Positioning System), Bluetooth, and LoRa (Long Range) radios cope with interference.

## Modes

- **Learn:** twelve short lessons with live signals and constellation diagrams, from a single carrier and analog against digital to QAM with noise, bit error rates, OFDM subcarriers, echoes and Wi-Fi rates, spread spectrum (chips, codes, hopping, and chirps), and all schemes side by side, each with a task you can check. Point at or tap any underlined term for its definition, or open the Glossary. A Real links page shows what phones, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, broadcast, cable, and fiber use, and why.
- **Calculator:** see any bits in any scheme, with the rates, the bandwidth, the working for every number, the effect of noise, and commands to see your own Wi-Fi link's rate.
- **Challenge:** fresh questions at three levels, badges, and a completion code.

## How to use it

Download `modulation-visualizer.html` and open it in any browser; it works offline.
