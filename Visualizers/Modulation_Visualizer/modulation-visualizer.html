What you will be able to do

  • Tell from a signal whether ASK (Amplitude Shift Keying), FSK (Frequency Shift Keying), or PSK (Phase Shift Keying) sent it
  • Find bits per symbol, and turn a symbol rate into a bit rate and back
  • Read a constellation diagram and Gray-coded bits
  • Decode DPSK (Differential Phase Shift Keying) from its phase changes
  • Explain why QAM (Quadrature Amplitude Modulation) needs a cleaner signal than PSK
  • Work out a Wi-Fi rate and see your own link's rate with a command

About this app. It draws ideal signals: every symbol holds a whole number of carrier cycles, and the pulses are not filtered. Every scheme is drawn at the same average power, so you can compare how close its points sit. Noise is simulated, and the same settings always give the same noise. Progress is kept in this browser only.
