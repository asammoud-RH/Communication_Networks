About this app

TCP Visualizer works offline in any current browser. It simulates one TCP connection between Host A (the client) and Host B (the server) over a path with a fixed delay and link rate, where you choose which segments are lost. The sender is limited only by the receiver's advertised window: the congestion window, slow start, and the other parts of congestion control are left out. The retransmission timer follows RFC (Request for Comments) 6298, with a 1-second minimum unless you choose Linux's 200 ms. Loss is found by a timeout or by three duplicate ACKs, with SACK (Selective Acknowledgment) when both sides allow it. Linux also uses newer loss detection (tail loss probes and RACK, Recent ACKnowledgment), so a real capture may show a resend sooner. The capture is taken at Host A, with Wireshark's relative sequence numbers and labels; timestamp values are left out. Progress and badges are kept in this browser only.
