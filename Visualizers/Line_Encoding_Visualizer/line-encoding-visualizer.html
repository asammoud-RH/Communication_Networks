What you will be able to do

  • Draw and read NRZ-L (Non-Return to Zero, Level), NRZI (Non-Return to Zero, Invert on ones), and Manchester signals
  • Explain why long runs of the same bit cause baseline wander and loss of the clock
  • Use three-level codes to spot errors on the line
  • Apply the B8ZS (Bipolar with 8-Zeros Substitution) and HDB3 (High-Density Bipolar 3-zeros) rules
  • Relate bit rate, baud rate, and efficiency
  • Find your own link's speed and the line code it uses

About this app. It draws ideal signals: square edges, no noise, and a receiver that reads the middle of each bit time (or each half for Manchester codes). Two-level codes use a high and a low level; three-level codes use positive, zero, and negative. Progress is kept in this browser only.
