What you will be able to do

  • Read a prefix length and its subnet mask
  • Find the network address, broadcast address, and host range
  • Tell whether two addresses are in the same subnet
  • Predict a ping when two hosts use different masks
  • Put one host in two subnets
  • Join and leave a subnet on Linux and Windows
