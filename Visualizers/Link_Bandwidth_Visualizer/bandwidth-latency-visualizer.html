      Your badges

      About this app. Bandwidth and Latency Visualizer works offline in any current browser. It models latency as propagation (distance ÷ signal speed) plus transmit (size ÷ bandwidth) plus queuing delay. Signal speeds are 2.0 × 10⁸ m/s in fiber, 2.3 × 10⁸ m/s in copper cable, and 3.0 × 10⁸ m/s for radio and in a vacuum. Bandwidth uses decimal units (1 Mbps, megabits per second, is 10⁶ bits per second). Sizes are decimal (KB, MB) unless you choose binary units (KiB, MiB). Transfer time is one RTT (round-trip time) plus the transmit time; real TCP (Transmission Control Protocol) transfers also spend time on the connection handshake and slow start. Switches and routers store and forward whole packets. Real ping times also include the time the hosts take to answer, which this model leaves out. Progress and badges are kept in this browser only.
